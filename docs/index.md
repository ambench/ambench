# AM-Bench

AM-Bench is an Isaac Lab extension for configuring and evaluating aerial-manipulation environments, robot profiles, control pipelines, physical effects, datasets, and supported policies. Visit the [project website](https://ambench.github.io/) for the research overview, paper, results, media, authors, and contact information.

## Get started

- [Install AM-Bench](getting-started/installation.md) in the supported Isaac Lab environment.
- [Verify the installation](getting-started/first-run.md) with one bounded headless simulation.
- [Find an environment](getting-started/find-an-environment.md) by inspecting the live registry.

## Use AM-Bench

- [Run an environment](workflows/run-environment.md) headlessly, with a window, or with video recording.
- [Collect demonstrations](workflows/collect-demos.md) with a scripted policy or teleoperation.
- [Validate a dataset](workflows/validate-data.md) before conversion or training.
- [Evaluate a supported policy](evaluation/index.md) with ACT, Diffusion Policy, or OpenPI.
- [Reproduce a research workflow](evaluation/reproduce.md) with an explicit data, training, and evaluation record.
